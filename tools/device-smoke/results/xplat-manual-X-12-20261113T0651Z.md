## Product behaviours the check had to follow (all named by the product message)
- an owner launching in its own team workspace needs an exec grant to itself on the machine (`pb team machine grant T <machine> --member <owner>`), else `pb machine list` in the team workspace is empty;
- every other device of an account must `pb env vault unlock` before its `pb env grants sync` (else `ENV vault changed`); the owner unlocks again after changing a team scope to re-seal the hosts;
- the unlocked vault locks after 15 minutes on a Linux machine without a secure store.
