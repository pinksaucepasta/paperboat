## Bugs found and fixed on the way (each with a test, all on main)
1. 15 of 237 creates failed 503 (postgres_serialization) at 8 parallel; 58 POST /v1/previews 503 and 1 operation_failed, 1 hung CLI create: the create ran ReconcileAgentTx over every live endpoint of the machine (O(N) read set vs renewals) -> assignNewPreviewTx.
2. 299 of 365, then 749 of 972, then 850 of 972 previews marked owner_lost: per-preview renewal every 10 s (36-100 transactions/s) -> batch endpoint, batches of 8 parallel items, 60 s for background previews.
3. decoratePreviewEndpoints re-planned a view-heavy statement on every GET and renewal: planning 326 ms vs 30 ms execution, 142 s of 190 s of PostgreSQL statement time in 8 minutes -> migration 372 (PL/pgSQL, force_generic_plan). After it: 812 consecutive creates, 0 failures, 1.2 creates/s, batch renewals 0.28 s average (before: 12-15 s, half timed out).
4. A QUIC carrier held 16 concurrent streams, not 1,024 (machine's incoming-stream credit 16).
5. A 1,000-stream burst lost ~1% of streams (hub queue 16, closed silently).
6. A managed preview stayed "connecting" because it waited for other accounts' self-hosted edges (decoratePreviewEndpoints lacked node_workspace_edge_eligible): would block every managed preview in prod once a customer edge exists.
7. Route permit now waits 1 s (not proven the cause of the near-limit failures seen twice in staging and not reproduced in three later runs).
