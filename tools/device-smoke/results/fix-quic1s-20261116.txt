FIX-QUIC1S: ~1.0 s lost on a fresh peer QUIC handshake. Two containers on pbvm2 (q1-a, q1-b), same account,
direct path. Per exec: `pb exec <peer> -- true`; quic_handshake from the client's redial_step diagnostics.

Reproduce (dev-d84c3dc52, main):
 warm carriers, 30 execs a->b: handshake 2-26 ms, no outlier.
 client carrier restarted before each exec (12): handshake 2-12 ms, no outlier.
 TARGET carrier restarted, exec 3 s later: 9 of 10 = quic_handshake 1001 ms failed (idle timeout) + retry 4-70 ms.
 TARGET restarted, exec 6/10 s later: 5 of 6 the same; 14 s: 1 of 3; 20 s and 60 s: 0 of 6.
Cause: the carrier keeps its keys and UDP port (41642 before and after the restart), so the signed contacts do not
change and nothing tells the client its WireGuard session died. The QUIC Initial goes out on dead keys, the restarted
target drops it, wireguard-go re-handshakes only after 15 s; QUIC gives up at its 1 s handshake idle timeout and the
retry's peer reset gets through. Tailscale learns a restart from a new disco key and calls MarkDevicePeerForHandshake
(ref tailscale ipn/ipnlocal/local.go:2579, wgengine/wgengine.go:215); our disco key is derived from the node key.

Fix (dev-bfb9766ea, branch build): every new authorized flow marks the peer for a handshake on its first send.
 TARGET restarted, exec 3 s later (a->b 12, b->a 6) and 6 s later (a->b 10): 28 execs,
 quic_handshake 204-210 ms (first PTO) x16, 606-610 ms (second PTO) x9, 1001 ms + retry x3 (first exec of each batch).
 Before: 17 of 19 at 1001 ms + retry.
 warm, 30 execs a->b: every handshake < 100 ms (unchanged).
Pair check on dev-bfb9766ea (`pb version` dev-bfb9766ea on q1-a and q1-b):
 pb exec q1-a->q1-b rc=0, q1-b->q1-a rc=0; pb connect q1-a->q1-b rc=0 echoed q1-b's hostname, q1-b->q1-a rc=0 echoed q1-a's.
Remaining gap: wireguard-go sends at most one initiation per RekeyTimeout (5 s), so an exec right after a target
restart can still wait out the 1 s timeout + retry (3 of 28 here).
