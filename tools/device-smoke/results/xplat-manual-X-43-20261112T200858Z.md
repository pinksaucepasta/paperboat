# X-43 first pass by hand on the shared fleet (lanex2), UTC 20261112 19:50-20:10 (IST 01:20-01:40 13 Nov)

Fleet builds: lanex2-l dev-64973afc6, apple dev-64973afc6, lanex2-w dev-85e45ee5c. A small origin (python, one per owner machine) answers any method and echoes the byte count. A public preview per owner (`pb preview <port> --background --json`), capture enabled with `pb preview inspect <preview> --enable --body --raw`, requests sent from oracle with curl, then `pb preview inspect` and `pb preview replay` run on the OTHER two machines.

## Part 1 PASS: captured requests are shown on the other two OSes with full headers and the CRLF body byte for byte
```
owner L:  viewer W and viewer M: record has 11 request headers (Forwarded, X-Forwarded-*, X-Request-Id, User-Agent...) and request_body 'a\r\nb\r\n\r\nc\r\n'
owner M:  viewer L and viewer W: request_body 'x\r\ny\r\n' exact
owner W:  viewer L and viewer M: request_body 'x\r\ny\r\n' exact
```
## Part 2 PASS: `pb preview replay <preview> <capture-id>` from the other two OSes reaches the origin of an L, M and W machine
```
owner L: replay from W, M and L (rc 0) -> the origin got the request again (a second record of the same URL, same CRLF body)
owner M: replay from L and W (rc 0), owner W: replay from L and M (rc 0): second record of the same URL each time
```
A replay is recorded as an ordinary capture of the same URL: `pb preview inspect` has no field that says "replay" (record keys: id, resource_*, method, url, headers, bodies, states, replay_ineligible, timestamps). The replay itself is audited separately and printed by `pb preview replay` (operation_id, idempotency_key).
## Part 3 FAIL (logged): binary, gzip and non-UTF-8 bodies are not captured
```
POST 1024 binary bytes, a gzip body with Content-Encoding: gzip, an iso-8859-1 body with charset=iso-8859-1 (owner L, viewers W and M):
  request_body_state=unsupported, request_body empty, state=unsupported (same on every viewer)
a UTF-8 text body (CRLF): request_body_state=complete
```
The row asks for bodies exactly as captured for binary, gzip and non-UTF-8 charset; the product keeps only valid text bodies and says `unsupported` for the rest.
## Other observation
`replay_ineligible=raw_expired` appeared on the Mac viewer about 10 min after the capture (raw retention expired) while the Windows viewer 2 min earlier showed ''. Replays done within a few minutes of the capture worked from every OS.
## Not run
The console's inspector page in a browser on another OS, `pb tunnel replay` and `pb tunnel inspect`, timezone of the viewer, capture storage bounds and removal with the preview, access refusal for other people (the F17 checks cover L; no cross-OS run), and resolving `localhost` for the replay origin.
